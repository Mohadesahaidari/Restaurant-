Our Story

Since 2015

A family table from Kabul

Arghawan is named after the flowering Judas tree that blooms every spring across Afghanistan. Our recipes come from family kitchens: slow-cooked qorma, hand-folded mantu and rice perfumed with cardamom.

In Afghan hospitality, a guest is a gift. We cook that way — generous plates for sharing, warm naan, and tea that never stops pouring.
